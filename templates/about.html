About MindCare

Understanding our mission to support mental health through technology

Our Mission

MindCare is dedicated to providing accessible mental health screening tools using advanced artificial intelligence. We believe that early detection and awareness are crucial steps toward better mental health outcomes.

What We Do

Our platform analyzes text patterns using sophisticated machine learning models to identify potential indicators of depression. While our tool is not a substitute for professional diagnosis, it serves as an early warning system to help individuals recognize when they might need professional support.

Technology Behind MindCare

We employ an ensemble of machine learning models including Support Vector Machines, Random Forest, and BiLSTM neural networks. Our system processes natural language to detect emotional patterns, sentiment indicators, and linguistic markers associated with depression.
